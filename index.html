Anemone Is My Home

A cozy clownfish aquarium for your Mac: as your desktop wallpaper, or in a window.

Contact

Questions, a problem, or an idea? Write to onikiienko.apps@gmail.com, in English or Ukrainian. It helps to say which version of macOS you have.

Questions

Where is the tank?

The game lives in the menu bar, as a small clownfish. Its menu puts the tank On the desktop, behind your icons, In a window, or Hidden. The shortcut ⌃⌥⌘A switches between the window and the desktop from any app; another one can be picked in Options ▸ Shortcut.

How do I look after the fish?

In the window, the buttons in the bottom left are your tools: food, a sponge for the glass, a net for selling fish, the shop and the logbook. Pick one up, click or drag in the tank to use it, and press esc to put it down. Feed the fish… and Clean the water… in the menu hand you the same tools.

A pack I bought is missing

Packs bought in the App Store come back on their own, on every Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. If one has not, open the menu bar clownfish's menu and choose Options ▸ Restore purchases.

What comes with me to a new Mac?

Packs bought in the App Store. The tank itself, with your fish, coins and packs bought with coins, is kept on the Mac you played on, and only comes along if you move that Mac's files across with Migration Assistant or a Time Machine backup.

Why is there sound only sometimes?

The tank only makes sound in the window, while you are using it, and never on the desktop. The speaker button in the window's bottom right, or Options ▸ Sound, turns it off.

Will it drain my battery?

The tank stops drawing whenever it is covered, hidden or the screen is locked, and on battery the desktop tank draws at a lower resolution.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring packs goes through the App Store.

Privacy
